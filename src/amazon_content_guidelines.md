# Amazon Listing Content Guidelines (Reference Copy — For Assignment Use)

> Note: This is a condensed, illustrative summary of publicly known Amazon
> listing style/content norms, assembled for this take-home exercise. Each
> rule below has a short ID (e.g. `AMZ-TITLE-01`) so the Ally skill can cite
> which rule a recommendation is based on. Treat this file as the "Amazon
> Content Guidelines PDF/MD" referenced in the assignment brief. Assume the
> same rules apply across all categories (Pet Supplies, Beverages, etc.).

---

## 1. Product Title

- **AMZ-TITLE-01 — Length:** Keep titles under **200 characters** (including spaces). Shorter, scannable titles (~80–150 characters) generally perform better on mobile.
- **AMZ-TITLE-02 — Structure:** Recommended pattern: `Brand + Model/Key Feature + Product Type + Size/Count/Color (if applicable)`.
- **AMZ-TITLE-03 — Capitalization:** Capitalize the first letter of each word (title case). Do **not** write in ALL CAPS.
- **AMZ-TITLE-04 — No promotional/subjective claims:** Do not include phrases like "Best", "#1", "Cheap", "Sale", "Free Shipping", "100% Guaranteed", or excessive punctuation (e.g., "!!!").
- **AMZ-TITLE-05 — No keyword stuffing:** Do not repeat the same keyword multiple times or cram unrelated search terms into the title.
- **AMZ-TITLE-06 — Required identifiers:** Include size, count, color, or flavor when the product has variants, so customers can distinguish this SKU from others.

## 2. Bullet Points (Key Product Features)

- **AMZ-BULLET-01 — Count:** Use up to **5 bullet points**.
- **AMZ-BULLET-02 — Length:** Keep each bullet under roughly **200–255 characters**; lead with the most important information first.
- **AMZ-BULLET-03 — Format:** Start each bullet with a short capitalized "header" phrase (e.g., "DURABLE DESIGN:") followed by a benefit-oriented sentence. Avoid ending with a period if using a fragment style, but be consistent.
- **AMZ-BULLET-04 — Focus on benefits, not just features:** Explain what the feature means for the customer (e.g., not just "rubber material" but "durable rubber construction stands up to aggressive chewing").
- **AMZ-BULLET-05 — No pricing, promotions, or shipping claims:** Do not mention price, discounts, deals, or shipping/guarantee terms in bullets — these belong in seller-controlled merchandising, not content.
- **AMZ-BULLET-06 — No ALL CAPS sentences:** Emphasis words may be capitalized (e.g., a short header), but full bullets should not be written in all caps.
- **AMZ-BULLET-07 — No unverifiable superlative/medical claims:** Avoid "best", "cures", "guaranteed", "clinically proven" (unless substantiated with certification), or claims about health/safety benefits that cannot be verified.

## 3. Product Description

- **AMZ-DESC-01 — Length:** Up to approximately **2,000 characters**. Use complete sentences and short paragraphs (avoid a single wall of text).
- **AMZ-DESC-02 — Content:** Expand on bullet points with use-case context, materials, care instructions, sizing guidance, and any relevant compliance/safety notes (e.g., "always supervise pets during use").
- **AMZ-DESC-03 — No contact info, external links, or promotional pricing:** Do not include phone numbers, email addresses, URLs to external websites, or pricing/promotional language ("buy now," "limited time," "free shipping").
- **AMZ-DESC-04 — No HTML in plain listings:** Do not paste raw HTML tags into the description field.
- **AMZ-DESC-05 — Consistent tone:** Avoid first-person seller voice ("we are proud to bring you...") — write in an informative, third-person, customer-facing tone.

## 4. Images

- **AMZ-IMG-01 — Minimum count:** Provide a minimum of **1 main image**; best practice is **5–7 images** including main + lifestyle + infographic + size/scale reference.
- **AMZ-IMG-02 — Main image requirements:** Pure white (RGB 255,255,255) background, product fills ~85% of frame, no text/logos/watermarks/props on the main image.
- **AMZ-IMG-03 — Resolution:** At least 1000px on the longest side to enable zoom functionality.

## 5. Prohibited / Restricted Content (All Fields)

- **AMZ-RESTRICT-01 — No pricing or promotional claims** anywhere in title, bullets, or description ("sale," "best price," "cheap," "discount," "free gift").
- **AMZ-RESTRICT-02 — No time-sensitive claims** ("order now," "while supplies last," "limited time offer").
- **AMZ-RESTRICT-03 — No guarantees/warranties language** unless it reflects an actual registered Amazon program (e.g., do not self-declare "100% money-back guarantee").
- **AMZ-RESTRICT-04 — No unverifiable superlatives** ("#1 best-selling," "best in the world") without substantiation.
- **AMZ-RESTRICT-05 — No reviews/testimonials referenced in content** ("5-star rated by customers," "customers love it").
- **AMZ-RESTRICT-06 — No competitor mentions** (brand names of other sellers/products) in title, bullets, or description.
- **AMZ-RESTRICT-07 — No seller/company contact information or external links.**

## 6. Keyword & Backend Search Terms (context, not customer-facing)

- **AMZ-KW-01:** Backend search terms should complement (not repeat) the front-end title/bullets, stay under the platform's character limit, and avoid duplicate or irrelevant terms. (Not directly assessed by this skill, but useful context if the model suggests backend keyword changes.)
